Three Smart Cafeteria Solutions

We offer three distinct checkout methods to match your cafeteria's needs.

AI Image Recognition

AI cameras instantly recognize dishes on the tray and auto-calculate the total. No special dishware needed — use your existing dishes as-is.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which checkout method is right for our cafeteria?
After an initial consultation, we will recommend the optimal method based on your cafeteria layout, menu style, and operational needs. You can also combine multiple methods in a single facility.
Can we keep using our existing dishes?
With the AI image recognition method, yes — no special dishware is needed. The RFID method requires dedicated RFID-embedded dishes. The weight-based method uses standard dishes with designated trays.
What payment methods are supported?
Face recognition, QR codes, IC cards (including transit cards), and credit cards are supported. Availability varies slightly by checkout method. Integration with existing employee IDs is also possible.
How long does implementation take?
From initial consultation to POC start is approximately 1–2 months. The POC period runs 1–3 months.
How is personal data (face recognition) handled?
We comply with Japan's Personal Information Protection Act with appropriate security measures. Face recognition is optional — QR and IC card payment work without it.
Can we combine multiple checkout methods in one cafeteria?
Yes. For example, you might use AI image recognition at the main counter and weight-based checkout at the salad bar. We design the optimal combination for your needs.
