Partnership with Newcapec Electronics

Bringing Smart Campus & Smart Cafeteria
Solutions to the Japanese Market

Izumo Soan has entered into a business partnership with Newcapec, a listed Chinese company, to deliver its smart campus and smart cafeteria products and solutions to the Japanese market.

Newcapec — A Leader in China's Smart Campus Market

Founded in 2000, Newcapec provides digital solutions that combine IC cards, QR codes and facial recognition for educational institutions, government bodies, public transportation, and enterprises.

Integrated Campus

Cafeterias, shops, facility use and access control across campus are unified into a single system, enabling a convenient environment where payments and identity verification work without carrying cash.

Proven Smart Cafeteria Track Record

Beyond smooth payments, Newcapec has extensive deployment experience supporting cafeteria operational efficiency, usage visibility and data-driven service improvement.

Multiple Authentication Methods

By combining IC cards, smartphones (QR codes) and facial recognition, the optimal payment and authentication method can be offered for each situation.

Extensive R&D Structure and Deployment Record

Newcapec has built R&D and service operations across China, providing solutions widely to educational institutions, enterprises, and the government and public sector. Key figures, per the company's materials, are as follows.

70+ Municipal Service Bases

10,000+ Enterprise Clients
300+ City-Related Clients

10+ Banking & Education Cloud Platforms

Drawing on ICT expertise cultivated over many years and large-scale deployment experience, Newcapec comprehensively supports smart campus, smart cafeteria, cashless payment, and authentication / access control.
