Frequently Asked Questions

Which method is right for us?
After learning about your cafeteria’s size, format and operations, we recommend the best method. Image recognition or RFID suits set-meal styles, while weight-based checkout suits buffet styles. Combining multiple methods is also possible.
Can we keep using our existing tableware?
With image recognition you can keep using existing tableware. RFID requires dedicated RFID-embedded tableware. Weight-based checkout uses a dedicated tray.
Which payment methods are supported?
We support facial recognition, QR code, IC cards (including transit cards) and credit cards. Supported options vary by method, so please contact us for details.
How long does deployment take?
From initial consultation to POC start is roughly 1–2 months, and the POC period is roughly 1–3 months.
Can the three methods be combined?
Yes. Multiple checkout methods can be combined within the same cafeteria — for example, image recognition at the set-meal corner and weight-based checkout at the buffet corner.
What are the conditions for the free POC?
It is for cafeteria operators such as companies, schools and hospitals. Guideline: Kanto area, 100+ meals per day. Equipment loan, installation and support during the POC are all free.
